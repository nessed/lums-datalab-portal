# Plain titles and summaries for review (round 2, 2026-11-15)

Every title and summary in `site/editorial/titles.json`, the file the site reads them from (it is the source of
truth; `site/scripts/draft-titles.mjs` only drafted it). All 925 are **drafts** for Ali's review.
Change a title or summary in the JSON file, set `"status": "approved"` when it is right, and the build checks it
(no digits, unique titles, at most 72 characters for a title and 130 for a summary, one sentence).

- **Hand-written**: 92 entries (headline and root series, budget summary lines, every receipts line, the national CPI and LFS headline series).
- **From templates, reviewed**: 833 entries (CPI groups by area and measure, LFS by measure, sex, province and area, budget function and sub-function lines by part of spending).
- Titles over forty characters: 430 (long tails that must name a measure, sex, province, area and definition, or a budget part and account).

Rows marked **REVIEW** are the ones where the plain words could be read as claiming more or less than the series is;
the notes below say why. Rules applied to whole families (earlier base, women and men, KP, new definition,
participation rate) are listed once below and their rows are not marked. Printed names are as the release stores them.

## Most in need of a look

- 'budgets to spend' covers budget and revised estimates; check the wording. (1 series, e.g. `fed.exp.total`: "Total federal spending")
- '(current)' means current spending on revenue account only (AGENTS.md section 7); the summary says so. Ali's 'Federal health spending' kept with the qualifier the rules require. (10 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.general_public_services`: "Federal general public services spending (current, revenue account)")
- Says '(current, revenue account)' in full because the release also holds a current capital-account line for this function; '(current)' alone would claim both. (2 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.general_public_services`: "Federal general public services spending (current, revenue account)")
- Title shortened to 'culture and religion'; recreation (sport) is in the summary and the printed name. Claims less, never more. (4 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.recreation_culture_and_religion`: "Federal culture and religion spending (current)")
- 'Executive, fiscal and external affairs': plain short form of a long printed head; check it reads right to an economist. (5 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.general_public_services.executive_legislative_financial_fiscal_external_affairs`: "Executive, fiscal and external affairs (current)")
- 'unclassified' for 'not elsewhere defined/classified', so it does not read like its sibling 'others'. (10 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.general_public_services.general_public_services_not_elsewhere_defined`: "General public services, unclassified (current)")
- 'Agriculture, food and irrigation' leaves out forestry and fishing in the title (they are in the summary and printed name). (6 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.economic_affairs.agriculture_food_irrigation_forestry_and_fishing`: "Agriculture, food and irrigation (current, revenue account)")
- 'Medicines and medical equipment' for 'medical products, appliances and equipment'. (1 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.health.medical_products_appliances_and_equipment`: "Medicines and medical equipment (current)")
- 'Higher education' for 'tertiary education affairs and services'. (3 series, e.g. `fed.exp.current.function.education_affairs_and_services.tertiary_education`: "Higher education (current)")
- Plain words for a budget-accounting line; check it does not over-explain. (4 series, e.g. `fed.rev.total_resources`: "Total federal resources")
- 'Federal tax revenue': what the line holds changes between editions; the summary points to About instead of claiming one meaning. (1 series, e.g. `fed.rev.tax`: "Federal tax revenue")
- A line listed under a different head is a different series; the title says which head. (5 series, e.g. `fed.rev.tax.indirect.petroleum_levy`: "Petroleum levy (under indirect taxes)")
- 'borrowing' for 'debt receipts'. The two bank lines are kept apart until a person checks the label (AGENTS.md section 7). (4 series, e.g. `fed.rev.capital.domestic_debt_net`: "Domestic borrowing, net")
- 'Household goods' for 'furnishing and household equipment maintenance'. (16 series, e.g. `cpi.national.group.furnishing_household.b2015_16`: "Household goods price index")
- 'Health care' for the CPI health group, so it is not confused with federal health spending; summary says 'medicines and health services'. (16 series, e.g. `cpi.national.group.health.b2015_16`: "Health care price index")
- 'Miscellaneous goods' for 'miscellaneous goods and services'. (16 series, e.g. `cpi.national.group.miscellaneous.b2015_16`: "Miscellaneous goods price index")
- Plain 'Inflation' is the national year-on-year change on the current base. Is that the one reading Ali wants for the bare word? (1 series, e.g. `cpi.national.yoy.general`: "Inflation")
- 'earlier base' for the 2007-08 base table, which prints no area (D27): never called national or urban. (51 series, e.g. `cpi.general.b2007_08`: "Consumer price index, earlier base")
- 'women' and 'men' cover everyone aged ten and over, as the survey counts them. (60 series, e.g. `lfs.unemployment_rate.male.total`: "Unemployment rate, men")
- 'Participation rate' is the refined rate (people aged ten and over); the crude rate says 'Crude'. (90 series, e.g. `lfs.lfpr_refined.total.total`: "Participation rate")
- 'KP' in the title for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (saves sixteen characters); the summary spells it out. (81 series, e.g. `lfs.lfpr_refined.kp.total.total`: "Participation rate, KP")
- '(new definition)' instead of the brief's '(new international definition)', to keep titles short; the summary says 'new international definition'. Never joined to the 13th ICLS series. (204 series, e.g. `lfs.lfpr_refined.total.total.icls19`: "Participation rate (new definition)")
- Industry shortened in the title ('trade', 'transport and communication', 'community services'); full division in the summary. (18 series, e.g. `lfs.employment_share.industry.wholesale_retail_trade.total.total`: "Share of workers in trade")
