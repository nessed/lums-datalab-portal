## Audit (before any change)

Baseline: `npm run build` green, 2,032 static pages in 2 min 35 s. Routes captured at 390, 820
and 1280 px: home, browse, three series (health with the devolution break; the CPI 2007-08 general
index, which is all gaps; the CPI 2015-16 general index), three receipts (a tier-A ABS value, a
CPI value, a "not published by the government" gap; no disputed observation exists in this
release yet), sources, one source, methods, releases, demo, about, 404. `/compare` and `/data`
do not exist and render the 404.

### What already works (keep it)
- The substance is right and honest: every value links to a receipt; gaps say which kind; the
  receipt states the evidence in a sentence, shows the printed text beside the parsed value, the
  tier, the file's verification in words, the SHA-256, the run id and the extractor. Copy is plain
  and specific, with no marketing language.
- The data layer (`lib/data.ts`) is the only reader of the release; values stay decimal strings;
  unpublished observations never carry a value. Status and gap wording is centralised in
  `lib/format.ts`.
- Charts never join across a missing period or a break; every point links to its receipt.
- Citations are two-part (government document + portal release) in APA, BibTeX and RIS.

### What looks generic
The home page structure (hero sentence, three CTAs, stat boxes, three cards); badge pills on
every cell; rounded bordered boxes around every block (cite, chart, callouts, tables); the system
font. None of it is offensive, but nothing says "reference work" and the eye has no anchor on
any page.
